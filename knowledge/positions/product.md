# Product — Position Knowledge

This file is compact institutional memory for **future occupants of the position**. It survives employee replacement.

## Durable lessons

- Separate observed problems from proposed solutions. Preserve evidence for why work was prioritized, deferred, promoted or killed.

## Promotion / cleanup

- Add only evidence-backed, role-relevant lessons that should survive a staffing change.
- Move normative rules into policy/ADR/runbooks.
- Remove or revise stale lessons instead of accumulating history forever.
- Do not store secrets, raw conversations, private reasoning, or changing operational facts here.
