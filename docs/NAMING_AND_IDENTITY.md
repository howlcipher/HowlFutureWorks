# HowlFutureWorks Identity and Naming

## Canonical identity

- Organization: **HowlFutureWorks**
- Repository/package slug: **`howl-future-works`**
- Ecosystem: **Howl**
- Persistent workforce runtime at initial deployment: **Grok Bot**
- Coding/execution providers: replaceable; currently Claude, Codex, AGY and future executors through common contracts.

`organization.yaml` is the machine-readable source of truth.

## Terminology

Use **member** informally for a HowlFutureWorks worker and **employee instance** in schemas/lifecycle records. Use **position** for a durable seat such as Product or Dev Lead. Use **role** for the responsibilities that may exist even without a persistent position.

The name intentionally does not include a model provider. Grok Bot is currently a deployment/runtime choice, not the organization's identity.

## Themed language

Subtle future-lab terminology may be used in human-facing project culture, but normative state, schemas, policies and runbooks use explicit engineering terms. Avoid renaming canonical concepts such as branches, experiments, approvals or incidents into themed vocabulary that would reduce clarity.
