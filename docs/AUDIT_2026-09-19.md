## Design findings corrected
1. **Missing workforce identity layer** — roles/positions and individual Bot instances were conflated.
2. **Offboarding too thin** — retirement guidance did not preserve enough handoff/context/tenure detail and conflated worker firing with position retirement.
3. **Institutional memory incomplete** — no structured employee lifecycle, contribution or curated context records.
4. **No succession/onboarding packet model** — replacements could easily inherit either too little or an unsafe/raw amount of predecessor context.
5. **Runbooks were placeholders** — expanded core operational runbooks into trigger/procedure/record/exit criteria.
6. **Charter Markdown formatting** — Google Docs export introduced split bold markers/HTML escapes; normalized for repo readability.
7. **Distribution was manual** — added repeatable `make dist` / `make verify-dist` tooling.
8. **CI hardening** — explicit read-only workflow permissions, checkout credential persistence disabled, timeout and dependency cache added.

## Workforce model added
Positions outlive workers. Workers may be hired, suspended, reassigned, terminated/fired, retired, laid off, replaced, provider-retired or rehired while historical records remain queryable.

## Verification expectation
Every future distribution should be produced from a clean committed tree and verified from both a fresh ZIP extraction and a fresh bundle clone.
