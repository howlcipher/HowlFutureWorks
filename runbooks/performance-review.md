# Performance Review

## Trigger

Use after a meaningful body of work, before consequential replacement decisions, or when repeated failures/cost/policy behavior indicate a fit problem. A critical security or policy incident may instead use immediate suspension first.

## Procedure

1. Identify the employee ID, position, review period and role-specific expectations.
2. Gather evidence from accepted/rejected tasks, verifier results, retries, costs, incidents, contribution records and handoffs.
3. Separate **worker fit** from a bad task definition, broken process, unsuitable provider/model, or flawed position design.
4. Compare outcomes to the role's documented responsibilities and relevant evals; do not score personality or speculate about internal mental state.
5. Choose the least disruptive evidence-supported outcome: continue, adjust instructions/skills, narrow scope, change provider/model, reassign, suspend, replace, or separate.
6. Record the review under `workforce/people/<employee>/reviews/` using `templates/performance-review.md`.
7. For replacement/separation, follow the applicable workforce runbook and preserve context/contributions.

## Exit criteria

- Review conclusions cite evidence.
- Follow-up owner/action is explicit.
- Any staffing change is separately authorized and recorded.
