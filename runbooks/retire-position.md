# Retire a Persistent Position

Retiring a position changes organizational design; it is **not** the same operation as firing the current worker.

## Procedure

1. Confirm why the durable ownership is no longer needed or is being absorbed elsewhere.
2. Identify all active occupants and separately complete reassignment/separation as appropriate.
3. Move stable position knowledge into the successor position or company knowledge when still relevant.
4. Reassign routines, queues, approvals, CODEOWNERS-like ownership and operational dependencies.
5. Deprecate the position first when a transition window is useful.
6. Update `bots/manifest.yaml` through reviewed organizational change.
7. Disable the live Bot position only after active work and staffing are clear.
8. Preserve all former employee, contribution, event, report and ADR history.
9. Record why the position was retired and what now owns its responsibilities.

## Must not do

- Delete former employee records.
- Rewrite historical attribution.
- Retire a position merely to evade its approval boundary.
