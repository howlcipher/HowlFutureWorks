# Emergency Suspend a Bot Employee

Use when immediate containment is more important than completing a normal separation.

1. Stop new tasks and explicitly pause/disable write-capable routines for the employee. **Do not rely on hiding the Bot; hiding does not pause routines.**
2. Disable/revoke employee-scoped temporary access where possible and review shared-computer sessions/sign-ins that may outlive Bot suspension or deletion.
3. Preserve logs, task envelopes, routine definitions, relevant recent routine-run evidence and current work state.
4. Record a `suspended` event and incident reference.
5. Reassign urgent work.
6. Investigate without letting the suspended employee modify its own evidence or policy.
7. Decide: reinstate with evidence, reassign, replace, or terminate.
8. Record the decision and verification.

Suspension is an organizational and operational state. Platform hide/delete controls are not substitutes for policy enforcement, routine containment, or credential revocation.
